Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our affiliate program

How do I get started?+

Fill out the application form via the Apply Now button. Our team reviews every application, and once approved you'll receive your unique tracking links and access to your affiliate dashboard.

Which commission structure should I choose?+

RevShare (40%) is ideal for maximizing long-term earnings from engaged users who make repeat payments. CPA (up to $40, with top rates reserved for top geos and quality affiliates) works great for paid traffic and quick campaigns. Not sure? We'll help you choose based on your traffic type.

Can I switch between RevShare and CPA?+

Yes. You can switch at any time, or run a mix of CPA and RevShare links simultaneously to suit your strategy.

When do I get paid?+

Payouts are issued monthly once you reach the minimum payout threshold. Your affiliate manager will confirm the exact schedule and payment methods when you're approved.

How does attribution work?+

We use last-touch attribution at sign-up. If a user clicks your link and creates an account, that user is credited to you — even if they come back later in another session to subscribe.

What kind of traffic is accepted?+

Most traffic sources are accepted, including social media, content sites, PPC, email marketing, Reddit, Discord, and Telegram. Adult traffic is welcome. We do not accept incentivized traffic, bots, or fraudulent sources.

Do you provide marketing materials?+

Yes. Approved affiliates get access to banners in standard sizes, landing pages, and text links — all regularly updated and built to convert.

Can I promote on adult sites?+

Absolutely. Adult traffic is fully accepted and encouraged — many of our top affiliates promote on adult content platforms with excellent results.

Why should I promote Nectar AI?+

We're building the best AI companion platform on the market. Constant product improvements, premium visual quality, and immersive chat, image, and video features mean your traffic converts better and users stay subscribed longer. Better user experience means better EPC for you.
