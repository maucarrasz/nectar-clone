Welcome to Nectar AI

Nectar AI is a platform for adults 18 and older. Chat with AI companions, fictional characters with AI-generated replies. You can also create your own companions and generate images and videos of them.

Start here

  1. Go to nectar.ai and confirm your age by tapping I'm over 18.

  2. Open ☰ → Discover and choose a companion.

  3. Select Start Chat. When prompted, you may log in or sign up with email, Google, or another option.

  4. Read the companion’s greeting, their opening message, and reply with something they can react to.

    • For example: “I just got in from the rain. Can I sit here while I dry off?”
      If the reply follows your setup, keep the conversation going. If it misses your intent, clarify one detail in your next message.

See Your First Chat for more tips.

Already using Nectar? Jump to Making Your Chat Better, Creating a Companion, or Image Generation.

What you can do

  • Chat with an existing companion or one you create.

  • Generate images and videos of your companions.

  • Use voice messages and calls to listen to replies or talk with your companion.

  • Create a companion with your choice of appearance, personality, and backstory.

  • Explore fantasies, ready-made stories with a setting and a role for you.

  • Try group chats with multiple companions in one conversation.

The guides below explain how to use each feature.

How it works

Nectar runs in your browser on desktop and mobile. No app download is needed.

Features and usage limits depend on your plan. Some actions use credits: Nectar’s in-app currency. Check the displayed price before confirming a paid action.

See Plans & Pricing for current details.

Add Nectar to your home screen

On your phone, you can add Nectar to your home screen.

  • iPhone: Open nectar.ai in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

  • Android: Open nectar.ai in Chrome. Tap the 3-dot menu, then Add to Home screen.

Getting help

Start with Troubleshooting and Getting Help.

    • Chat Toolkit is a Chrome extension for saving drafts, searching memories, and exporting chats.

    • Nectar Wiki is an in-depth guide to features, prompts, and tips, maintained by the community.

  • Events: Watch the Community tab and Discord for workshops, contests, and AMAs.

  • Ask the community: Post in #prompt-discussion for help with characters and prompts, or in #support for other questions.
